# Specification: Homepage UI/UX Revamp

## Objective
Revise the homepage (`index.html`) to drastically improve the UX and UI, giving it a true professional polish while maintaining the general established style.

## Scope
- **Hero Section:** Improve the initial impact by refining spacing and typography, and adding a professional tagline to immediately communicate your value proposition.
- **Main Content (Summary):** Implement a "Modern & Airy" layout. Remove the dated, heavy borders (`border-[1.5rem]`) and replace them with subtle shadows or soft background colors. Convert the large block of italicized text into standard, readable typography with proper spacing.
- **Call to Actions (CTAs):** Add prominent "Contact Me" and "Read More (About)" buttons to guide users effectively, complementing the existing "View Resume" CTA.
- **Employer Logos:** Reorganize the "Previous Employers" sections into a clean, uniform grid with subtle hover effects to ensure visual balance and a polished corporate feel.

## Non-Goals
- Changing the established brand colors or typography fonts defined in the Tailwind config.
- Refactoring the navigation or footer components (already addressed in a previous track).
